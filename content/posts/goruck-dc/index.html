GORUCK City Ruck: DC

Published

May 4, 2025

I did the GoRuck 12 mile City ruck in DC.

At check in, you go to a table and photograph a list of waypoints. During the briefing we learn that you are allowed to visit the waypoints in any order, which naturally raises the question of what order it’s optimal to visit them in.

Let me pause to define some potentially unfamiliar or ambiguous terms:

Rucking is walking around with weights on.1

“Optimal” has to refer to some objective function. Since I told you I’m walking around with weights (20 lbs and three liters of water), an obvious candidate meaning is that I would like to be doing that for as little time as possible, which I would accomplish by taking the shortest route.
