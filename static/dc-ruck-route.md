# GORUCK DC - Optimal 12-Mile Route Plan

## Start/End Point:
- **Gravelly Point** (George Washington Memorial Pkwy, Arlington, VA 22202)
  - *Team photo required before departure*
  - *Check-in with remote support mandatory*

## Optimal Route Sequence:

### Segment 1: Arlington to National Mall
1. **Gravelly Point** → Cross the Potomac River via Mount Vernon Trail to DC
2. **Lincoln Memorial** (2 Lincoln Memorial Cir NW, DC 20002)
   - *Take picture at the TOP of the stairs in front of Lincoln statue*
3. **Korean War Veterans Memorial** (900 Ohio Dr SW, DC 20024)
   - *Take picture in front of one or more steel statues*
4. **Thomas Jefferson Memorial** (16 E Basin Dr SW, DC 20024)
   - *Take team picture from inside the memorial in front of the statue*
5. **Washington Monument** (2 15th St NW, DC 20024)
   - *Take picture in front of the memorial*

### Segment 2: National Mall Central
6. **Martin Luther King, Jr. National Memorial** (1964 Independence Ave SW, DC 20003)
   - *Take picture in front of Martin Luther King, Jr.*
7. **National Museum of American History** (1300 Constitution Ave NW, DC 20560)
   - *Take picture in front of the fountain in front of the museum*
8. **56 Signers of the Declaration of Independence Memorial** (1836 Constitution Ave NW, DC 20006)
   - *Take picture on the small island after crossing the bridge*

### Segment 3: Federal Triangle & Capitol Area
9. **National Archives** (700 Pennsylvania Avenue NW, DC 20408)
   - *Take picture in front of the columns of the building*
10. **Capitol Building** (101-113 East Capitol St NE, DC 20543)
    - *Take picture in front of The Capitol*
11. **National Law Enforcement Officers Memorial** (444 E St NW, DC 20001)
    - *Take picture in front of the lion across from the lion cubs*

### Segment 4: White House Area and Return
12. **White House** (1600 Pennsylvania Ave NW, DC 20001)
    - *Take picture from Lafayette Square with the White House in the background*
13. **National Seabee Memorial** (1 Memorial Ave, Fort Myer, VA 22211)
    - *Take picture in front of the sculpture*
14. **Return to Gravelly Point**

## Total Distance: Approximately 12 miles

## Route Considerations:
- This route forms a logical loop that minimizes backtracking
- Groups monuments by geographic proximity to reduce unnecessary walking
- Accounts for crossing the Potomac River twice (at the beginning and end)
- Follows major pedestrian-friendly paths and National Mall walkways
- Takes advantage of the concentrated nature of DC monuments

## Photo Requirements:
Remember to take all required photos at each waypoint with the specified compositions. Each photo should include your team and use the hashtags mentioned in the instructions.

## Safety Notes:
- Stay hydrated throughout the 12-mile course
- Use crosswalks and obey pedestrian signals
- Be respectful at all memorials and monuments
- Maintain team cohesion throughout the route
